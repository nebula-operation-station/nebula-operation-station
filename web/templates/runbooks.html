Runbooks

A runbook is your own script, run on hosts as an operation like the built-in actions. Every save is a new, unchangeable version; an operation always runs the exact version it was planned with. The executor treats a runbook script it does not know as at least HIGH (your security key every time) until you trust that version on the executor host, and its Safety Scanner can raise the risk further.
